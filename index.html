Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you accept walk-ins?

No — METZ MODS is appointment-only. It's how we give every build our full attention instead of juggling multiple vehicles at once. Call or send us a WhatsApp message to book a slot.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

Everything from tuner cars and sport compacts to trucks and SUVs headed off-road. If it can be modified, we can probably help.

Do you do both lift kits and lowering kits?

Yes — whether you're chasing a lower stance or more ground clearance, our suspension work covers both ends of the spectrum.

How long does a modification take?

It depends on scope. A bolt-on exhaust or intake can be a same-day appointment; a full lift kit or custom bumper build takes longer. We'll give you a timeline at your consultation.

Do installed parts come with a warranty?

Yes. Our installation work is warranty-backed, and most parts carry their own manufacturer warranty on top of that.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp us with your vehicle and what you're looking to do, and we'll follow up to talk pricing and scheduling.
